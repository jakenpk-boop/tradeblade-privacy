TradeBlade — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Summary

TradeBlade is an offline tool. Everything you enter — jobs, clients, materials, photos, calculations and company settings — is stored only on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your data.

No account, no server

TradeBlade has no sign-in and talks to no server. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking code in the app, and no third-party SDKs. It works with the phone in airplane mode.

Photos and camera

Photos you add or capture are saved on your device and attached to your jobs. The camera and photo library are used only at the moment you choose to add a photo, and the images go nowhere else.

Ask runs on your iPhone

The Ask feature uses Apple's on-device model. Your question, the numbers in it and the answer never leave the phone, and nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.

Backups and exports

When you export a Quote, an Invoice or a backup file, it is created on your device and goes only where you send it — Files, iCloud Drive, Mail, wherever you choose. That file is yours and we never see it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it and the new version appears both in the app and at the published web address.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@tradeblade.app.
